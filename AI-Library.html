4 · Investigation protocol & quality gate

Uncertainty visibleSeparate fact, allegation, inference, and forecast.

Minimum validation checklist

  • Check date, author, provenance, and original publication.
  • Trace quotes, images, video, and statistics to primary material where possible.
  • Check whether apparently separate outlets repeat one source.
  • Seek disconfirming evidence and relevant missing perspectives.
  • Label uncertainty; do not convert absence of evidence into proof of absence.
  • Recheck fast-moving claims immediately before publication.

Editorial safety rules

  • Allegations about named people or groups need careful attribution and a fair opportunity to respond where appropriate.
  • Do not publish private personal data merely because it is accessible.
  • For conflict reporting, avoid treating state or armed-group claims as independently verified facts.
  • Do not expose sources, vulnerable people, or operational details that create foreseeable harm.
  • Record what was not checked, not just what was found.
