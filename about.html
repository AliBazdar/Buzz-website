Candesartan Reference

Class: Angiotensin II Receptor Blocker (ARB)

Starting Dose: 8 mg once daily (typical for hypertension)

Max Dose: 32 mg once daily

Expected BP Reduction: ~8-12/5-8 mmHg at 8-16 mg

Key Monitoring: Potassium, creatinine, eGFR at baseline and 1-2 weeks after initiation/dose change

Contraindications: Pregnancy, bilateral renal artery stenosis, hyperkalemia

Risk of hyperkalemia increases with concurrent potassium-sparing diuretics, potassium supplements, or CKD. Monitor serum potassium closely.
