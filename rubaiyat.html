Archive Overview

This archive collects Rubaiyat attributed to Omar Khayyam and related poets, with notes on authenticity, transmission, and interpretive layers.

Each entry includes the original Persian text, transliteration, one or more translations, and commentary on themes, imagery, and epistemic horizons.
